## Design Context

### Users
Property/parking-lot owners and on-site staff at Nepali residential/commercial complexes (e.g. "Sallyan House") who run a paid parking operation as part of property management. They check this dashboard multiple times a day to track revenue, occupancy, and payment reconciliation — often while also handling walk-up ticketing at a gate. Not necessarily technical users. Currency is NRs (Nepali Rupee); the wider system also tracks the Bikram Sambat calendar alongside AD dates.

### Brand Personality
Modern, energetic, ambitious — a fast-growing, confident product feel rather than a stale back-office tool. Reference direction: DaVinci Resolve and other professional/pro-tool software — dense information layout, dark-panel command-center aesthetic, precise data readouts, segmented controls, monospace numerals for tabular data. Anti-reference: generic templated SaaS dashboard look (identical icon+heading+text card grids, pastel gradients, default shadcn gray-on-gray).

### Aesthetic Direction
Pro-tool / command-center visual language: dark mode as a first-class real theme (not an afterthought or a simple invert), high information density, crisp typographic hierarchy with tabular/mono numerals for money and counts, segmented pill controls, and the sharp-but-refined panel language of professional editing software (Resolve/Premiere-style). Should read as "serious operational software," not a marketing site. The existing light theme (shadcn zinc/oklch grays + teal-600 accent, Geist Variable font) is the light-mode baseline — dark mode should feel like its own deliberately-designed pro-tool counterpart, not just flipped lightness values.

### Design Principles
1. **Numbers are the hero** — money, occupancy %, and counts always render in monospace/tabular-nums with a clear weight hierarchy (big bold headline figures, smaller muted context lines).
2. **Dark mode is a designed destination, not a CSS invert** — build real dark tokens with intentional contrast and accent tuning, not just flipped oklch lightness.
3. **Density over whitespace-for-its-own-sake** — this is a working tool checked many times a day; favor tight, scannable layouts over generous marketing-style spacing.
4. **Segmented/pill controls and tab-like navigation over dropdowns** — matches the pro-tool reference and keeps frequent actions one click away.
5. **No decorative gradients, glow, or glassmorphism** — confidence comes from precision and restraint, not visual effects.
